HATUA YA 5: NAMECHEAP DNS SETTINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. Login: https://www.namecheap.com
2. Account → Domain List
3. Kando ya sadofaraji.com → bonyeza "Manage"
4. Tab ya "Advanced DNS"
5. FUTA records zote za zamani za A na CNAME za sadofaraji.com
6. Ongeza records mpya:

   TYPE:      A Record
   Host:      @
   Value:     76.76.21.21
   TTL:       Automatic

   TYPE:      CNAME Record
   Host:      www
   Value:     cname.vercel-dns.com
   TTL:       Automatic

7. (Hiari) Ongeza redirect www → non-www:
   TYPE:      URL Redirect Record
   Host:      www
   Value:     https://sadofaraji.com
   Redirect:  Permanent (301)

8. Bonyeza: Save Changes (checkmark ya kijani kando)
