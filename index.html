About the Author

About Sado Faraji

SADO FARAJI is an author and historian from Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. His work blends history, faith, and narrative — building a bridge between Islamic scholarship, cultural critique, and allegorical fiction.

Through novels, plays, and scholarly works, he explores the collision between tradition and modernity, betrayal and loyalty, love and life. His writing carries allegorical force — written with the intent to be heard across generations.

Writing from the margins of empire, he recovers the stories that colonial states, religious authorities, and political dynasties sought to erase or conceal.
